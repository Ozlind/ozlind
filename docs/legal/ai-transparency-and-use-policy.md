## What Ozlind does
Ozlind provides AI-assisted features such as chat and, where enabled, document-related workflows, saved artifacts, and web search. Exact feature availability may change.

## AI limitations
- AI may produce false, misleading, biased, incomplete, or outdated output.
- Citations, links, code, and calculations may be wrong or may not support the generated statement.
- Uploaded content can contain malicious instructions or inaccurate information.
- AI output is not professional medical, legal, financial, or emergency advice.
- Review generated code before running it and never paste untrusted code into production without review.

## How requests are processed
To fulfil a request, Ozlind may send the prompt, relevant conversation context, and user-selected files to the model provider configured for the selected feature. Do not use Ozlind to violate law, infringe rights, expose private information without authorization, or facilitate harm. Do not treat AI output as verified fact without checking it when accuracy matters.
